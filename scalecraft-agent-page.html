No coding needed.

One command sets everything up. You just answer a few questions about your business.

1
Get a dedicated WhatsApp number
Any prepaid SIM (Airtel/Jio) that isn't already on WhatsApp. Install WhatsApp on a second phone or use your dual SIM slot. Takes 10 minutes.
2
Get a free Gemini API key
Sign in at aistudio.google.com with your Google account. Create a free API key. This is the AI brain that powers your agent - costs ₹50–₹200/month based on usage.
3
Run one command on your server
On any VPS (AWS Lightsail (Mumbai), DigitalOcean) or your local Mac/Linux machine, run:

Answer 7 questions about your business
The installer asks your business name, what you sell, price range, product link, and team contact. Takes 5 minutes. Your agent's personality is generated automatically.

Scan the QR code - you're live
Scan the QR with your bot phone. Your agent starts running immediately. Send "Hi" from another phone to test. It works 24/7 from this moment forward.
